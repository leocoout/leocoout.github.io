Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

"time to post bb" is a personal, single-user iOS application that helps a content creator schedule and publish their own videos to their own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube) and view the performance statistics of those videos.

Data we collect

The app does not operate any backend server and does not collect, sell, or share any personal data. All information handled by the app stays on the user's device:

  • Account access tokens obtained when you connect your own social accounts are stored exclusively in the iOS Keychain on the device.
  • Videos and post details (titles, captions, hashtags, schedules) are stored locally on the device.
  • Video statistics (view, like, comment, and share counts of your own posts) are fetched from the platforms' official APIs and stored locally on the device.

How platform data is used

Data received from TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube APIs is used solely to display your own content and its statistics to you, and to upload videos you explicitly choose to publish. It is never transmitted to any third party or server.

Data deletion

Disconnecting an account inside the app deletes its tokens from the device. Deleting the app removes all remaining data. You may also revoke the app's access at any time from the security settings of the connected platform.
